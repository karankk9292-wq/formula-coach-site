Formula Coach Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Summary

Formula Coach does not collect, store or share any personal information. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your lesson progress, streak, test results, settings and purchase status locally on your iPhone using the device's standard storage. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to any server owned by us.

Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple shares anonymised purchase information with us so we know how many people bought the app. See Apple's privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Notifications

Daily reminders are scheduled locally on your iPhone and never sent from a server. You can turn them off at any time in the app or in your iPhone's Settings.

Analytics and tracking

None. The app has no analytics SDK, no tracking, no advertising identifiers.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated.
